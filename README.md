# Callosa de Segura in voxels

Callosa de Segura and its sierra in 1 m voxels, from the huerta up to the Pico del Águila. You can fly over it, change the date, hour and weather, or walk around.

**Live:** https://raulbr99.github.io/callosa-voxels/ (best on a computer with a recent browser).

The castle ruins are drawn by procedure from photos, so their size is an estimate. The facades are single-colour boxes.
